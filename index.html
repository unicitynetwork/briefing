Payment Transfer State Machine Fixes
A major cluster of fixes hardens the TransferMachine's handling of edge cases in payment lifecycle. PR #858 ensures in-process converge reuses the outcome of a settled attempt rather than redoing work (#844), #855 fixes conflict reporting on resume so it's only reported once abort is confirmed (#846), and #853 closes a gap where failures after inclusion proof match were incorrectly treated as clean (#839). #849 refuses named spend/burn cleanly during paused spending (#838), #851 decouples SENT history posting from close-gating (#842), and #852 is a breaking change removing DIRECT:// as a valid payment recipient along with its helpers (#847). #850 refactors outcome helpers and SENT history out of TransferMachine for cleaner separation of concerns.

Nostr Relay Infrastructure Rollouts
Two sequential relay rollouts (#51, #52) bump the tokens relay to new SHAs, with #51 also widening the grace period from 300s to 900s to reduce disruption during rollout. PR #27 adds an authorization fix (UNIP-01) ensuring only a nametag's owner can reference it from kind-30078 events, closing a spoofing vector. These changes improve both operational safety of relay deployments and protocol-level integrity of nametag ownership.
