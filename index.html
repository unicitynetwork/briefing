Enrolment Protocol Hardening in Codewall
jvsteiner landed a cluster of fixes to the enrolment flow: #41 retries interrupted enrolment and polls immediately, #42 and #44 recover enrolment after a lost response, and #37 verifies principal lifecycle isolation on the installer. #48 further retries temporary enrolment policy conflicts. Together these close a series of edge cases where machine enrolment could stall or leave inconsistent state, making the onboarding path far more resilient.

On the Oracle side, #71 prepares the Oracle 2026.9.2 release while #69 and #65 fix authenticated Distro apply and keep Codex setup and dynamic capabilities usable.

Enrolment Policy Selection and Enforcement Gaps
A series of fixes closes gaps where enrolment policy assignment could silently fail or enforce nothing: #350 adds a policy selector to the 'Enrol a machine' token window (#349), #356 disables endpoint/enrol policy dropdown options lacking an edge artefact instead of allowing a later 409 (#355), and #351 ensures unassigned/fallback endpoints don't enforce a policy with no published edge artefact (#348). #346 adds tenant confirmation before minting enrolment tokens, and #347 recovers enrolment after a lost response, mirroring the Astrid fix. Together these harden the enrolment UX and prevent misconfigured or silently permissive policy states.
